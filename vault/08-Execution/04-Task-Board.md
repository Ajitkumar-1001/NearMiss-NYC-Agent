> §23 item 9 — *"Historical correlation is not causal proof"*; §23 item 10 keeps

> *sibling* of risk rather than an input — the contract is the part that is
> right. Note also that `risk.py:135` sets `vulnerable_user=1.0` for every pair
> — a floor on any VRU conflict, not a discriminator, because FR-009 has
> already filtered to vehicle–VRU pairs.
> **What survived the rejection:** history is **displayed beside** the score
> (S-4, C-10), and the missing multiplier stays a code task — **C-1 above,
> unchanged.** Routing the conflict zone to a candidate **gate** instead of a
> scoring factor is a **proposal pending a decision**, not an adopted design
> change: the [[00-Source-of-Truth-PRD|PRD]] specifies no zone geometry
